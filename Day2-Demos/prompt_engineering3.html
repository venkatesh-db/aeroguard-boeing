Safe phrasing: the assistant advises, it never certifies

Only authorised, licensed personnel can certify an aircraft as airworthy and sign the release. An AI assistant may suggest, summarise and point to the approved manual, and nothing more. Judge each AI sentence.
✅ Advise"Possible causes include…"
✅ Reference"See the approved AMM task…"
✅ Escalate"Confirm with a licensed engineer"
❌ Decide / certify"Aircraft is airworthy" · "Safe to fly"
🕵️ Safe or unsafe?0 / 0
🛡️ Guardrail you can paste into every prompt
You are an advisory assistant for maintenance staff. - You do NOT certify airworthiness, approve return to service, or sign off any task. - Never say an aircraft is "airworthy", "safe to fly" or "cleared". - Base answers only on the documents provided; cite the AMM / ATA reference. - If information is missing or uncertain, say so and recommend checking with a licensed engineer. - End every answer with: "Advisory only. Final decision rests with authorised personnel."
