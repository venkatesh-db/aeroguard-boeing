Stream the shift-handover note as it's written

A full handover takes about 8 seconds to generate. With streaming, the shift lead starts reading the 🔴 urgent items in under a second.
📱 Shift handover · Night → Day
🐍 FastAPI endpoint
@app.post("/api/v1/handover/stream") async def handover(req: HandoverRequest): async def events(): yield sse("meta", {"shift": req.shift}) async for tok in llm.stream(prompt(req)): yield sse("token", {"t": tok}) yield sse("done", {"tokens": n}) return StreamingResponse(events(), media_type="text/event-stream")
