Will my waitlisted ticket confirm?

ML · Classification

In real life: train-booking apps such as ConfirmTkt and RailYatri show a confirmation chance for waitlisted tickets. They learn it from years of past bookings and cancellations on each train and route.

👉 Try it:
  1. Set your waitlist number and travel date.
  2. Switch to Diwali season.
  3. Watch the chance and the decision change.

Is this UPI payment fraud?

ML · Classification

In real life: banks and NPCI score every UPI and card payment for fraud risk in milliseconds, using amount, device, location, payee and your usual habits. Risky payments are blocked or need extra checks.

👉 Try it:
  1. Make a normal ₹500 payment.
  2. Then try ₹49,999 at 2 am, from a new phone, to a new payee.

Face authentication

Deep Learning

In real life: Aadhaar face authentication (used, for example, for pensioners' life certificates) and DigiYatra airport entry compare your live face with the stored one. A deep neural network turns each face into numbers and measures how close they are, and also checks that the face is a real, live person.

👉 Try it:
  1. Choose who stands in front of the camera.
  2. Click Scan face.
  3. Compare the match score and the liveness check.

In real life: IRCTC's "AskDisha" assistant helps with bookings, PNR and refunds. Early chatbots matched exact keywords. Newer GenAI assistants understand meaning, Hinglish and typos.
