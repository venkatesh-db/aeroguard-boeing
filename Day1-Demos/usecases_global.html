Deep Learning · Regression

AI that cools a data centre

ML · Control

In real life: DeepMind's ML system controlled cooling in Google data centres. Google reported up to 40% less energy used for cooling. It predicts heat in advance instead of reacting after servers get hot.

👉 Try it:
  1. Press Run 24 hours.
  2. Compare the rule-based thermostat with the ML controller.
  3. Try a heatwave day.

What a self-driving car sees

Deep Learning · Vision

In real life: Waymo runs driverless taxis for paying riders in several US cities. Deep neural networks turn camera, lidar and radar data into labelled objects, many times every second, then decide how to drive.

👉 Try it:
  1. Turn on AI vision to see the detected objects.
  2. Make the pedestrian step into the road.
  3. Change the light or turn on night mode.
