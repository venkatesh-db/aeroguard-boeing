Live aircraft health monitoring

MLRules

In real life: services such as Boeing Airplane Health Management, Rolls-Royce engine health monitoring and GE Aerospace engine analytics stream sensor data from aircraft in flight. Engineers on the ground can see a problem before the aircraft lands and have the part and team ready.

👉 Try it:
  1. Watch the normal sensor readings.
  2. Click Inject bearing wear. It starts small and grows.
  3. See when ML spots it, compared with a simple fixed-limit rule.

Fleet predictive maintenance

ML · Regression

In real life: platforms such as Airbus Skywise and Lufthansa Technik AVIATAR predict the remaining useful life of parts across a whole fleet. Parts are swapped at a planned stop, before they fail, instead of on a fixed calendar.

👉 Try it:
  1. Move the planning window slider.
  2. Switch between Fixed schedule and ML prediction.
  3. Compare failures and wasted parts.

AI flight routing

ML + optimisation

In real life: Alaska Airlines worked with Airspace Intelligence ("Flyways") to suggest better routes to dispatchers using weather, winds and traffic. The result was less fuel and fewer delays. A human dispatcher still approves every route.

👉 Try it:
  1. Move the storm and change the wind.
  2. Watch the AI pick the cheapest safe route.
  3. Compare its fuel with the straight route.
Grey = straight route · Blue dashed = other options considered · Green = AI's choice

GenAI customer agent

GenAI

In real life: Air India launched "AI.g", a generative-AI virtual agent that answers customer questions in several languages, any time of day. Airlines use agents like this for baggage, rebooking and flight-status questions.

👉 Try it:
  1. Tap a question, or type your own.
  2. Try asking in Hindi.
  3. Note how it understands the meaning, not exact keywords.
