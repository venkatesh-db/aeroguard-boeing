① Embeddings: turn text into "meaning coordinates"

📖 An embedding is a list of numbers that captures the meaning of a text. Similar meaning means nearby numbers. Indexing = embedding every document once and storing the vectors so they can be searched fast.

② Chunking, overlap & metadata

📖 Long documents are cut into chunks before embedding. Overlap repeats a little text between neighbouring chunks so facts on a boundary aren't cut in half. Metadata (source, section, page) travels with each chunk so answers can cite it and be filtered.

③ Vector databases & hybrid search (BM25 + vectors)

📖 A vector database (FAISS, ChromaDB…) finds the nearest vectors fast. BM25 is classic keyword search: great for exact codes and part numbers. Hybrid blends both, so you catch meaning and exact terms.

⑤ LangChain fundamentals: snap the building blocks together

📖 LangChain gives ready-made blocks: loaders read files, splitters chunk them, embeddings + vector stores index them, retrievers fetch chunks, prompts + LLM answer, and chains connect it all. Tools let the LLM call functions. Click the blocks in order.

⑥ Guardrails: no-answer policy · PII redaction · prompt-injection awareness

📖 Guardrails are the safety rules around RAG: say "I don't know" when the documents don't contain the answer, hide personal data, and never obey instructions hidden inside documents.
